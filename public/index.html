FROM SCATTERED TO SORTED

Your workspace, in three steps.

Start with your Google account. Bring the rest together as you go.

  1. 01

    Connect your first Drive

    Select Connect Google Drive, sign in with Google, and approve the requested access. This is your primary sign-in account.

  2. 02

    Bring your other Drives

    Choose Connect another Google Drive in the workspace. Your available storage appears together in the sidebar.

  3. 03

    Upload. Find. Get on with it.

    Use + New or drag files into the workspace. Organize them in folders, search by name, and download when you need them.

YOUR DRIVES STAY YOURS

Connected through Google.
Stored in your Drives.

You sign in through Google OAuth. Drivepool uses the drive.file permission for files you use with the app. Your uploaded files are stored in your connected Google Drives.

Drivepool is an independent app, not a Google product. It does not add storage to your Google accounts.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
bring it together.

What exactly does Drivepool do?

Drivepool brings multiple personal Google Drive accounts into one file workspace. You can see combined available storage and manage files uploaded through Drivepool, while the files remain in your Google Drives.

Will all my existing Google Drive files appear?

The workspace lists files uploaded through Drivepool. It is not a full mirror of every existing file in your Google Drive accounts.

Does it give me extra storage?

It combines the available space you already have in your connected accounts. Each account keeps its own Google storage quota; Drivepool does not create extra storage or change your Google plan.

What happens when one Drive does not have enough space?

Drivepool chooses available connected storage for an upload. Large files can be split across Drives and reassembled when downloaded through Drivepool. Keep the accounts containing those parts connected to retrieve the complete file.

Which account should I use to sign in again?

Use the first Google account you connected. That primary account opens your workspace. Additional connected accounts contribute storage but do not sign you into the same workspace.

Can I use Drivepool on my phone?

Yes. Open the website in your mobile browser. On a supported browser, choose Install app. On iPhone, use Safari’s Share menu and Add to Home Screen. An internet connection is required for Drive access.
